1. Hybrid search. Embeddings find chunks with the same meaning, but they can miss exact terms like an error code,
 a product name or “ERR-4021”. Keyword search (such as BM25) finds exact words but misses paraphrases. 
 Hybrid search runs both and merges the results.

2. Reranking. Retrieval is fast but rough. A common pattern is to fetch the top 20 chunks cheaply, 
then have a slower, more accurate reranker model re-score just those 20 against the question, 
and keep the best 3 for the prompt. Fast filtering followed by careful ranking.

3. Evaluation. You can’t improve a RAG system you can’t measure. 
You build a small test set of questions with known answers and check two things separately:

Retrieval: did the right chunk appear in the top results? (This is recall, the metric from earlier, applied to chunks.)
Generation: is the answer faithful to the retrieved text, with nothing invented?
