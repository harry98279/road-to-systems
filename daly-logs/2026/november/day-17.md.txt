1. Ingest:   split your documents into small chunks, embed each chunk, store the vectors
2. Retrieve: embed the user's question, find the chunks with the highest cosine similarity
3. Generate: prompt = "Answer using only this context: <top chunks>  Question: <question>"
Context window: 500 pages may not fit into the model’s token limit at all.
Cost and speed: you pay per token, and more tokens means a slower reply.
Accuracy: a few relevant paragraphs lead to better answers than hundreds of irrelevant pages,
where the model can miss or mix up details.
