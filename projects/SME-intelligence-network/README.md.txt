
# SME Intelligence Network

## Overview

SME Intelligence Network is a Pakistan-focused
financial intelligence platform designed for banks
and financial institutions.

The platform helps financial institutions analyse
SME financial statements, evaluate credit risk,
monitor business performance and identify
financing opportunities.

## Problem

Many SMEs have limited access to formal financing
because their financial information is fragmented,
inconsistent or difficult to evaluate.

Banks also spend significant time manually
processing financial documents and assessing
business financial health.

## Our Solution

A centralised financial intelligence platform
that provides:

1. Automated financial statement extraction
2. Financial ratio analysis
3. SME credit intelligence
4. Financial distress indicators
5. Portfolio monitoring
6. Business valuation
7. Working capital intelligence

## Initial Target Customers

- Commercial banks
- Islamic banks
- Development finance institutions
- Financial institutions

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Flask
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- React.js
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Scikit-learn

## Development Approach

The platform will be developed incrementally,
starting with financial statement extraction
and automated financial analysis.

## Long-Term Vision

Build a financial intelligence infrastructure
that improves SME financial transparency and
access to capital in Pakistan.
