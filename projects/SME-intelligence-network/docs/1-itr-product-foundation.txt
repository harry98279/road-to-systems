
# Day 01 — Product Foundation

## 1. Product Name
SME Intelligence Network

## 2. Product Category
B2B Financial Technology (FinTech)

## 3. Target Market
Pakistan

## 4. Primary Customers
- Commercial Banks
- Islamic Banks
- Development Finance Institutions
- Financial Institutions

## 5. Core Problem

Banks face challenges when assessing SME businesses:

- Financial documents are inconsistent.
- Financial analysis requires manual work.
- SME creditworthiness is difficult to assess.
- Historical business performance is fragmented.
- Monitoring existing SME borrowers is difficult.

## 6. Our Solution

Build an integrated financial intelligence platform
that automates SME financial analysis and provides
explainable credit-risk decision support.

## 7. Core Product Modules

1. Financial Intelligence Engine
2. Credit Intelligence Engine
3. Portfolio Monitoring Engine
4. Business Valuation Engine
5. Working Capital Intelligence
6. Capital Opportunity Network

## 8. Initial MVP

The first version will include:

- SME profile management
- Financial document uploads
- Financial data extraction
- Accounting validation
- Financial ratio calculations
- Financial health reports
- Basic credit-risk indicators
- Bank analyst dashboard

## 9. Business Model

B2B Enterprise SaaS.

Revenue sources:

- Annual subscriptions
- Per-assessment charges
- Portfolio monitoring subscriptions
- Enterprise integrations
- Advanced analytics

## 10. Initial Technology

Frontend: React.js
Backend: Python + Flask
Database: PostgreSQL
Data Processing: Pandas + NumPy
Machine Learning: Scikit-learn
Visualisation: Plotly

## 11. Development Principles

- Financial accuracy before AI complexity.
- Explainable results.
- Secure financial data.
- Human oversight in credit decisions.
- Modular architecture.
- Regulatory compliance.

## 12. Long-Term Vision

Become a trusted SME financial intelligence
infrastructure provider in Pakistan and
eventually expand into international markets.
