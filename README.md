# 🚀 Road to Systems

### My Journey Towards Becoming an AI/ML Engineer & Systems Engineer.

**Road to Systems** is my personal journey of developing the technical knowledge, engineering mindset, and practical experience required to enter the world of AI/ML Engineering and related engineering roles.

This repository serves as a public record of my progress — documenting what I learn, what I build, the problems I solve, and the systems I experiment with along the way.

My focus is to move beyond simply using existing technologies and develop a deeper understanding of how intelligent systems are designed, developed, integrated, and deployed.
